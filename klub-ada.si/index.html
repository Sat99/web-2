Testimonials from our members and partners

Become a Partner

"I first met Simon, the co-founder of the Ada Club. She was a finalist for Engineer of the Year 2023, and her youthful, enthusiastic, and highly skilled approach really stood out to me. Since I was also part of the engineering circle where we have the Danfoss Women in STEM team, we established contact with Simon and agreed to participate in a roundtable discussion on the topic of "Code for Success: Career Planning Workshop in Technology!".

The Ada Club inspires both young and older people. They are a motivated team with many ideas that they easily realize in successful events and projects. They do things from the heart. That's why I support them and wish them success in the future!"

Tamara Štumperger, Supplier Quality System Senior Manager @ Danfoss

"I have only attended one event so far, and it was very well organized, with current topics and amazing guests. I met women with similar experiences, which gave me the motivation to continue learning and working. I'm happy that there is such a community here where I can find support and advice for my career path.

I also recommend joining the Discord community for more networking and knowledge sharing."

Maruša Kerpan, Automation Engineer @ Domel

"I have been attending Ada Club events since the very beginning.

Participating in workshops has given me the opportunity to connect with other women in technology, acquire new knowledge, and above all, gain self-confidence for continuing my career.

Jana Vovk, Student @ FRI

"A few months ago, I needed advice on finding my first job, and yes, it was worth it for all the help, support, and advice I received. Both our events were very enjoyable times in the company of ambitious and positive-minded women in technology. The Discord community of the Ada Club is a treasure trove for those seeking motivation, employment, education, or just a good book.

My experience with the Ada Club is completely positive!"

Nika Likar, UX Designer @ Omnia8

"With the help of the Ada Club, I learned about various interesting events that allowed me to connect with and make new friends with amazing women from the IT world. We met in Berlin and spent a wonderful day together at the conference.

It really is a nice feeling to be part of a community where people support each other in the same way, understand each other, and encourage each other."

Tamara Demir, Web Developer @ ENKI
